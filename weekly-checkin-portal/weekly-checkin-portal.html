📖 How to Use the Weekly Check-In Portal

A step-by-step guide for regional sales leaders and global ops teams to complete the weekly forecast submission cycle.

Step 01

Open the Portal

Access the portal every Monday morning. The Dashboard tab shows the current cycle status and which regions have submitted.

Step 02

Submit Bookings

Go to Submit Bookings, select your region, enter CQ actuals and NQ pipeline by sub-region. Save Draft first, then Submit when ready.

Step 03

Submit Revenue

Go to Submit Revenue, select your region, enter QTD actuals and full-quarter forecast. Flag any variance >5% vs target.

Step 04

Book & Bill

Enter short-cycle deal commitments in the B&B Plan tab. These are deals expected to book and close within the same quarter.

Step 05

Generate Narrative ✨

In the Narratives tab, click Regenerate to get an AI-drafted summary. Review, edit as needed, and add Risks & Opportunities.

Step 06

Final Submission

Global Ops reviews all regions in Final Submission, approves ready regions, sends back those needing revision, then locks for CRO.

What does each status badge mean?

Pending — not yet submitted this week. Submitted — data entered, awaiting ops review. Approved — ops confirmed, included in global submission. Needs Revision — ops has flagged an issue; regional VP must resubmit.

What is the submission deadline?

Bookings and Revenue must be submitted by Thursday 18:00 local time. Narratives by Thursday EOD. Final Submission is locked Friday morning for the CRO briefing.

Can I submit on behalf of another region?

Only Global Ops admins can override region submissions. Contact your ops partner if you need to submit on behalf of a colleague.

What is the Word Export?

The Export Word button generates a formatted Word document with your submission data and narrative. In production, it uses docx.js for browser-native generation — no server required.

How does the AI narrative work?

The ✨ Regenerate button sends your submitted numbers and prior-week narrative to the AI API, which drafts a structured business summary with key highlights and risks. You review and edit before submitting.
