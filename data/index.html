Quality decisions you can inspect

Preserved uncertainty

  • 1,425 opportunities have no account. They remain in the data as missing.
  • 2,089 open opportunities have no close value or close date. These values remain null.
  • “First observed purchase” means first within this extract. It does not establish a genuinely new customer.

Documented repairs

  • 1,480 product references normalized from GTXPro to GTX Pro.
  • 12 account sectors corrected from technolgy to technology.
  • 8,800 opportunities before and after joining; no duplicated opportunity IDs.
